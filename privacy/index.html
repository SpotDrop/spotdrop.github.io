SpotDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

SpotDrop is an iPhone app for finding places to eat and drink through friends. This page explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and what you can ask us to do. We wrote it to be read.

Who we are

SpotDrop decides how your information is used (the "controller" in UK data protection law). You can reach us at arthurcamb@hotmail.com.

What we collect, and why

InformationWhat it is used for
Your account. Email address, display name and username. Optionally a short bio and a profile photo.Signing you in and letting friends find you. Your display name, username, bio and photo are visible to other SpotDrop members. A profile photo is stored at a web address that anyone with the link can open.
Your Spots and friends. Spots you save (been there or want to try), who can see them (friends or only you), friend requests and friendships, and Spots you send to a friend with an optional note of up to 200 characters.Showing your Spots on your map and profile, to the friends you chose, and delivering shares. We store a reference to the place (its Google Place ID), not a copy of the venue's details.
Your location, only if you allow it, and only while you use the app.Centring the map on you and finding places near you. Your location is sent to Google to get nearby places. We do not store it on our servers.
Notifications, only if you allow them. A code Apple gives your iPhone for notifications, which types you switched on, and when you last opened Discover.Sending the daily drop, the reminder, shared Spots and friend requests, and skipping the reminder if you already looked.
How the app is used. App opens, whether you viewed or finished the daily drop, which Discover clips you watched, watched to the end or could not play, and when you saved, shared or opened a Spot from Discover. Also whether you accepted the notification prompt and when you tapped a notification. Each event carries a random ID the app creates for your installation and the app version, and your account when you are signed in.Seeing what works and fixing what does not. We may share totals with venues or creators (for example how many people saved a Spot), never anything that identifies you.
Error reports. The error message, technical details, app version, your iPhone's system version, and your account when signed in.Finding and fixing faults.

Our reasons under UK data protection law: we need your account details and Spots to provide the app (contract); we ask your permission for location and notifications (consent); and we use the usage and error information to run and improve the app (legitimate interests).

Who else handles your information

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, photo storage and notification sending. Our project runs in London.
  • Google provides the map and place information (Google Maps and Places). Requests from your iPhone reach Google directly and include your IP address and, when you look for places near you, your location. See Google's privacy policy.
  • TikTok. Discover plays videos with TikTok's own player. While a video loads, TikTok may collect information as described in TikTok's privacy policy. The videos belong to their creators.
  • Apple delivers notifications and distributes the app.

We do not sell your information, show advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and Spots until you delete your account. We keep usage events and error reports only for as long as we need them to run and improve the app, and we delete the ones linked to your account when you ask us to delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can change your name, username, bio and photo in the app, and switch each type of notification on or off under Edit profile. You can also turn off location or notifications for SpotDrop in iPhone Settings.

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or stop using it for a purpose, and you can ask for it in a portable form. To delete your account and everything linked to it, open Edit profile in the app and tap Delete account. This removes your profile, photo, Spots, friendships, shares and notification settings straight away, along with the usage events and error reports linked to your account. If you cannot use the app, email us from the address on your account and we will do it within 30 days. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

SpotDrop is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app and update the date above.
