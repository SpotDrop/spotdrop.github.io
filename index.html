SpotDrop

Find London's best places to eat and drink through the people you trust: your friends.

iPhone app · London · now in beta

What SpotDrop does

Your map of Spots

Save the restaurants, bars and cafés you love and see them all on one map of London.

Saved by friends

See which friends saved a place before you go, and what they said about it.

Share with a note

Send a Spot to a friend with a personal note. "You have to try the pasta here."

The daily Drop

Every day, three short video clips of London venues, picked by our team.

Where we are

SpotDrop is in beta on Apple's TestFlight, with a first group of testers in London. We are building it actively and plan to launch on the App Store soon.

Restaurants, partners and anyone who wants to try the beta: we would love to hear from you.
